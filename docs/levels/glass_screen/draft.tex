\section{What the level asks}\label{sec:asks}

\leveldescription

This draft does not pass the comparison. The numbers below are the rectangle
calculation. See \code{NOTES.md}.

One positive ion is launched along $+x$. A fixed charge sits above a glass
slab of permittivity $\val{eps}$. The reference adds one charge below the
launch. The slab's bound charge is
$\sigma = (\lambda / 2\pi)\, \bar E_n$ with $\lambda = \val{lam}$.

\section{The calculation}\label{sec:derivation}

The rectangles and the mirror are \xeqref{deflection_plates}{eq:rectangle}.
The normal-field condition is averaged on four Gauss points. The panel's own
normal field is left out of the average. One constant per body enforces
neutrality of the bound charge. There are $\val{nPanels}$ upper rectangles.
The centroid residual of that system is $\val{solveRes}$.

Inside the slab the field is $\val{eInside}$, the fraction $\val{insideRel}$
of the point-charge field $\val{eInsideC}$. On the launch line it is
$\val{eGlass}$, the fraction $\val{screenRel}$ of $\val{eCoulomb}$.

\section{Comparison with the game}\label{sec:comparison}

The mesh gap is $\val{errMesh}$ and the step gap is $\val{errStep}$. The
tolerance set from them is $\val{tolFlight}$.

The reference leaves through the \val{RefFace} edge at
$(\val{RefX}, \val{RefY})$. The game arrives in the detector. The ordinate
differs by $\val{cmpRefYDiff}$ and the abscissa by $\val{cmpRefXDiff}$. With
no player charge the abscissa differs by $\val{cmpBareXDiff}$. The same player
charge with the slab removed ends as \val{HitOutcome}.
